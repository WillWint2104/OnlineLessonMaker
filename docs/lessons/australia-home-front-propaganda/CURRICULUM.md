# Curriculum provenance and partial coverage

Checked 3 October 2026 against the live official NSW Curriculum site, rather than the superseded History structure.

- [History 7–10 Syllabus (2024): course overview](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/hsie/history-7-10-2024/overview/course) confirms implementation from 2027, 2026 preparation, Stage 5 Years 9–10 and the core Australia at war – WWII depth study.
- [Official Stage 5 outcomes](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/hsie/history-7-10-2024/outcomes) verified all four supplied outcome texts, including the full purposes/audiences/contexts qualification for communication.

The exact supplied official wording is retained in the editable lesson’s four curriculum records and displayed separately from authored mapping notes. Student learning intentions and activity wording are original classroom wording, not syllabus quotations. Outcome codes do not stand in for a claim of whole-outcome coverage.

| Outcome | Lesson evidence | Coverage limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HI5-SOU-01 | Selecting poster details, explaining persuasion and using both sources in an argument | Partial: two visual sources and a short argument; not all historical accounts, explanations or source types |
| HI5-INQ-01 | Observation/inference classification, usefulness judgement and proposals for corroboration | Partial: poster inquiry only; no independent archival research or actual reception study |
| HI5-CPP-01 | Comparing official messages, contexts and omissions in audience perspectives | Partial: two departments’ messages; not a representative survey of wartime perspectives |
| HI5-COM-01 | A 150–200 word historical argument with disciplinary terms and qualification | Partial: classroom audience and one response purpose; not the entire range of contexts/audiences/purposes |

Each record includes the authority, syllabus version, implementation source, level, depth study, exact outcome text, official URL, checked date, mapped skill IDs and a labelled author interpretation with partial coverage. No additional syllabus codes or official content bullet quotations are invented. The home-front focus is an author’s selection within the verified depth study, not a claim that this lesson fulfils all its content.
