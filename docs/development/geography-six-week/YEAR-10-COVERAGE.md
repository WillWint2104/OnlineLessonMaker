# Year 10 — Human Wellbeing: provisional six-week coverage matrix

**Provisional educational-design proposal for human review. No class completion is assumed.** 18 proposed one-hour periods. Week labels are relative teaching weeks, not dates or a software deadline. The pattern assumes three periods per week. These are flexible capacity scenarios, not confirmed timetables. Confirm starting points, interruptions and adopted syllabus before final allocation; do not assume any lesson has already been completed.

Source basis: the user's expanded topic/skills guidance and redesign direction, repository examples and candidate 2015 syllabus alignment. Original resources are in another conversation and are not needed to proceed. Every row is a proposed design, not a record of completed teaching. Outcome codes are provisional; GE5-7/8 concern inquiry/communication. See [sources and assumptions](README.md).

| Y10-16 / W6 | Comparing strategies to improve wellbeing | Which strategy fits the place, problem and evidence? | Compare strategies with consistent criteria and justify a place-specific judgement. | Sustainability, scale and interconnection; effectiveness, equity and durability. | GE5-2, GE5-6, GE5-7, GE5-8 |
| Y10-17 / W6 | A geographical wellbeing inquiry | What should be investigated before recommending action? | Frame a question, select evidence and justify a recommendation with limitations. | Inquiry and scale; acquire, process and communicate provenance-linked evidence. | GE5-1, GE5-2, GE5-6, GE5-7, GE5-8 |

## Independent and classroom alternatives

O1 = self-contained instructions/glossary, accessible source/table alternatives, a worked example, independent attempt before policy-controlled model/feedback, and completion/return instructions using the school-approved process. No new backend, durable autosave or submission feature is assumed. R1 = proposed relief pack with a 60-minute script, vocabulary, numbered source/question pages, expected responses/misconceptions, completion check and no-login fallback. These packs must still be authored and validated.

Default one-hour rhythm: 5-minute retrieval, 10-minute explanation/model, 10-minute source reading, 15-minute guided application, 15-minute independent work, 5-minute check. Adjust literacy/accessibility supports to the cohort. No extra homework period is silently counted.
