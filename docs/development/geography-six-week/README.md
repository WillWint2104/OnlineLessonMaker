# Six-week Geography production plan — provisional approval package

Prepared 24 October 2026 under the user's revised planning scope. **This is a curriculum and production proposal, not a research launch, authored lesson corpus or six-week software-development deadline.** Online learner delivery remains the principal product.

The requirement is six teaching weeks for each course. **15 one-hour Year 9 periods and 18 one-hour Year 10 periods are provisional capacity scenarios**, not confirmed timetables. The two pilots test a reusable library; they do not replace whole-program planning. The intention is to redesign stronger learning experiences, not reproduce old worksheets or PowerPoints.

## Two delivery experiences: isolated design research

The learner player remains the production host. Stage A may propose a **separately isolated, JSON-informed classroom consumer** using the same lesson content and completed widget sources, with adapters where needed. This is a design question, not permission to add a presentation mode, new controls or a second learner engine.

The proposal must address projector demonstrations, whole-class investigations, structured notes/book work, controlled answer reveal, printable source/question worksheets, relief-teacher instructions and no-device teaching. It must distinguish authored explanation/assessment/model content from learner state and classroom view state; propose safe answer-inclusion/release rules, readable projection/print layouts, accessible alternatives, asset/provenance handling and offline/export limitations. A teacher working through an existing learner lesson is an immediate delivery alternative, not proof that a new presentation consumer exists.

No new runtime, registry, shared schema, exports, print pipeline or consumer contract is authorised. C11 implementation is deferred until separate architecture/integration scope and human approval. Do not duplicate widget implementations or contaminate the online player with presenter behaviour. Relief/printed packs described in the matrices are specifications still to author; no claim of ready-to-use worksheets is made.
