## Australian Curriculum

Authority: Australian Curriculum, Assessment and Reporting Authority. Current Version 9.0 Mathematics, Algebra. A national reference is supportable for Factorising, with distinct Year 9 and Year 10 scopes:

- [AC9M9A02 on the official Year 9 page](https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/resources/work-samples/mathematics/year-9/ws04-polynomial-math-art): monic factorising is explicitly included. This mapping applies to Skill 1 and does not claim non-monic coverage.
- [AC9M10A01 on the official Year 10 page](https://www.australiancurriculum.edu.au/resources/work-samples/mathematics/year-10/ws01-mathematics-assignment): wider expression manipulation. Skill 2 contributes to factorisation only; equation solving and the full exponent-law content are excluded. The record uses a labelled exact opening excerpt, not a purported full quotation.

These official pages reproduce the adopted content descriptions. Their work-sample artwork, photographs and student work were not copied. The 2021 review-consultation PDFs returned by search were rejected because they differ from the adopted Version 9 descriptions. A national straight-lines record was not added: the verified NSW Core entries suffice for this milestone, and no national equivalence is inferred.
