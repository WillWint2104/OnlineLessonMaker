Two values of x, worked separately, arriving at the same y.

Case A

Starting from x = 3

Question

Find the point on y = x2 where x = 3.

Worked solution

  1. Substitute the positive value.
    y = 32
  2. Evaluate.
    y = 9
Answer(3, 9)

Case B

Starting from x = −3

Question

Find the point on y = x2 where x = −3.

Worked solution

  1. Substitute the negative value.
    y = (−3)2
  2. Evaluate. Squaring removes the sign.
    y = 9
Answer(−3, 9)

Why the two agree

The two x-values sit at equal distance either side of the y-axis, and squaring sends both to the same y. That is what makes x = 0 the axis of symmetry.
