Reading a value from a described situation

Question

A ball is thrown straight up from the edge of a balcony. Its height above the ground, in metres, is modelled by h = 20 + 15t − 5t2, where t is the time in seconds after it leaves the hand.

The balcony rail is 20 metres above the ground, and the model is only trusted while the ball is above the ground.

Find the height of the ball two seconds after it is thrown, and state whether it is above or below the rail at that moment.

{{DIM:prompt}}

Worked solution

  1. Substitute t = 2.
    h = 20 + 15(2) − 5(2)2
  2. Evaluate.
    h = 20 + 30 − 20 = 30
Answer30 m — 10 m above the rail.
