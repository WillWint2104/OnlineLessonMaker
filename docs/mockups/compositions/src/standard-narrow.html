An ordinary worked example that needs no major representation.

Is the point on the curve?

Question

Decide whether (5, 24) lies on y = x2, and justify your answer.

Worked solution

  1. A point lies on the curve when its coordinates satisfy the rule. Substitute the x-value.
    y = 52
  2. Evaluate.
    y = 25
  3. Compare with the y-value the point claims.
    25 ≠ 24
AnswerNo — (5, 25) lies on the curve, so (5, 24) sits just below it.

What this shows

Testing a point is one substitution: the rule decides, not the picture.
