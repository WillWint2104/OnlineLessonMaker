Adversarial proof — a line of mathematics wider than the track it is in. Prose wraps; an equation does not. The track is not widened past its sibling's claim and the mathematics is not shrunk: the line takes its own horizontal scroll, exactly as an over-wide table already does in the workbook.

Expanding a product of three brackets

Question

Expand and simplify (x + 2)(x − 3)(2x + 5).

{{DIM:prompt}}

Worked solution

  1. Expand the first two brackets.
    x2 − x − 6
  2. Multiply every term of that result by every term of the third bracket. This line does not wrap, and it is not made smaller to fit — it scrolls.
    2x3 + 5x2 − 2x2 − 5x − 12x − 30 = 2x3 + (5 − 2)x2 − (5 + 12)x − 30
  3. Collect like terms.
    2x3 + 3x2 − 17x − 30
Answer2x3 + 3x2 − 17x − 30
{{DIM:solution}}
What this provesContent size changes feasibility, never the composition. An over-wide line does not promote this example to a different family, does not steal width from the question, and does not trigger a smaller type size. It is one region reporting that it cannot be set at this width, handled locally.
