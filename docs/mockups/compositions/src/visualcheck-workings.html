Working backwards: the y-value is known and the x-values have to be found.

Find x when y = 16

Question

Find the value(s) of x for which y = 16.

Worked solution

  1. Substitute the known y-value into the rule.
    16 = x2
  2. Take the square root of both sides. A positive number has two square roots.
    x = ±4
  3. Check both against the rule.
    42 = 16 and (−4)2 = 16
  4. The graph check on the next stage shows why there are two answers.
Answerx = 4 or x = −4.
