Thinking prompt

Is a number always smaller than its own square?

Square 6 and you get 36. Square −6 and you get 36 as well — the minus sign disappears, and both numbers land at the same height on the curve y = x2. Now try one half. Now try 1. Does squaring really push every number further out?

Test three numbers before you decide — one large, one between 0 and 1, one negative — then compare with the person beside you.
