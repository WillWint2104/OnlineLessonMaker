Key idea

Three side lengths build one triangle, and only one

So once you have matched all three sides of one triangle to the three sides of another, you are finished measuring. Every angle already agrees, and you may quote any angle from one triangle as the angle in the other.

It does not claim the two triangles sit the same way up — one may be turned or flipped over. Same shape and size, not the same position.
