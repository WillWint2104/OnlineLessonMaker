Triangle construction

Three sides are enough to fix a triangle

Nobody chose the angles here. They were settled the moment the three lengths were, and the construction simply reveals them. Read the three measures marked at A, B and C: each one is a consequence, not a decision.

That is the side–side–side test. Give the same three lengths to the whole class and every triangle that comes back is this one — turned over or spun around, never a different shape.

Try it: build the triangle yourself with ruler and compasses, then check your angles against the ones marked here.
