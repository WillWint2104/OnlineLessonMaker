Reading the graph

Dividing by 12 flattens the parabola: the curve needs 24 units of x to climb the same 6 units of y that y = x2 covers in about five.

y = x2 / 12

The line y = 3 still meets it twice, at x = −6 and x = 6, and those crossings are still mirror images about the y-axis. Flattening changes the scale, not the symmetry.

At equal unit scale this domain is three times wider than it is tall, so the figure is wide and the composition places it across the full measure with the reading beneath. Putting it in a side-by-side column would have made the plane small enough to stop being readable, which is a property of the figure — not of the room left over after the text.
