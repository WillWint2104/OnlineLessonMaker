A fraction, the long way

Question

Use the rule y = x2 to find y when x = −2/3, and say where the point sits relative to (−1, 1).

Worked solution

  1. Write the rule.
    y = x2
  2. Substitute, keeping the brackets so the sign and the whole fraction are squared together.
    y = (−2/3)2
  3. Squaring a fraction squares the numerator and the denominator separately.
    y = (−2)2 / 32
  4. Evaluate the numerator. A negative squared is positive.
    (−2)2 = 4
  5. Evaluate the denominator.
    32 = 9
  6. Write the value.
    y = 4/9
  7. Compare with 1. Since 4 < 9 the fraction is below 1, so the point sits lower than (−1, 1).
    4/9 ≈ 0.444 < 1
  8. Check the sign of the x-coordinate is unchanged — only y came from the squaring.
    (−2/3, 4/9)
Answery = 4/9, so (−2/3, 4/9) lies below and to the right of (−1, 1).

A decimal, in one step

Question

Find y when x = 0.2.

Worked solution

  1. Square it.
    y = 0.2 × 0.2 = 0.04
Answery = 0.04
