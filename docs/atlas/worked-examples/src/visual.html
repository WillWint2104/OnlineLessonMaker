Reading the graph

The horizontal line y = 9 meets the curve twice, and the two crossings are the values that square to 9.

x = −3 or x = 3

They sit at equal distances either side of the y-axis, which is the same fact as “squaring destroys the sign”, drawn.

This plane is balanced: at equal unit scale it is no taller than it is wide, so it is the one shape that makes a side-by-side composition work. A plane taller than it is wide leaves the reading column ending far above the plane’s foot, and takes the down subdesign instead. That is the figure’s own geometry deciding, not the length of this paragraph.
