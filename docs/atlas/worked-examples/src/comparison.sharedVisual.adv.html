Why the two agree

Dividing by 12 flattens the curve without moving its axis of symmetry, so the mirror pair survives: x and −x still reach the same height.

y = x2 ÷ 12

A shallow curve over a wide domain needs width to be read, so this figure takes the whole content region and the explanation sits beneath it. That is a designed subdesign, not a failure state.
