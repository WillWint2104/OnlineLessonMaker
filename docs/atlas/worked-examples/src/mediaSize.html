THE MISSING LAYER. Geometry can say what shape a plane must keep. It can never say how large that plane deserves to be — a supporting number-line, an ordinary worked-example graph and a major explanatory graph can all share one aspect ratio. mediaSize is authored, and it is the step between the media and its geometry.

Below: ONE figure at three authored sizes, then a second figure of a different shape at the same three. Read down each ladder — the size class moves, the geometry class and the composition family do not.
