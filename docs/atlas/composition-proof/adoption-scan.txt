THE SELECTION CHANGE, which no row comparison can show
  The shipping catalogue selects by (surface, aspect class). The blueprint layer selects by
  (presentationRole, geometryClass, surface). NO SHIPPING PAGE DECLARES A presentationRole,
  so adoption is not catalogue-only: every page carrying media has to author one. Pages that
  carry media:
