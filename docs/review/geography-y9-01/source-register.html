Estimated Resident Population (usual residents). The grid distributes SA1 estimates over residential address points, Indigenous Community Points or fallback SA1 centroid points, then sums into 1 km² cells.
measure
Modelled density, people per km²; NOT a growth rate, household count, migrant flow or urban/rural classification
geography
Australia, National Nested Grid; 2021 SA1/ASGS Edition 3 inputs; EPSG:3577. Lesson adaptation shows mainland and Tasmania, excludes distant-island margins.
classes
Modelled zero; Below 500; 500 to below 2,000; 2,000 to below 5,000; 5,000 to below 8,000; 8,000 or more
adaptation
Native JPEG reduced decode, proportional crop, resampling, state/territory region labels, title and verified legend added. No invented geometry or recolouring. Reduced cells blend: only broad patterns are assessed; exact per-cell numerical extraction is not supported.
comparability
Same 2024 reference/release as B, but grid cells and GCCSAs are different spatial units. No one-to-one equivalence between coloured cells and complete city boundaries; no density ranking calculated from B.
limitations
2024 ERP preliminary in this release; estimates may be revised. Modelled cell allocation and national-scale reduction limit precision. Grey is a model category, not proof of no community, activity, cultural connection or occupation. No causal/migration/job inference is established by the map.

B · Population change by capital city — four selected rows

producer
Australian Bureau of Statistics
release
Regional population 2023–24, 27 March 2025

https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/people/population/regional-population/2023-24
snapshot
sources/abs-regional-2023-24.html
data
sources/city-data.json
reference
ERP at 30 June 2024; change from 30 June 2023 to 30 June 2024
population
Usual-resident ERP. Foreign diplomatic personnel/families excluded. June 2024 preliminary in this release; June 2023 revised.
measure
Residents (count); added residents over one year (count change); percentage increase relative to the starting population (growth rate, rounded to 1 decimal place). Not urban share or density.
geography
Greater Capital City Statistical Areas (GCCSA), ASGS Edition 3. Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth only; not all capitals, all urban areas, or equal-area regions.
comparability
Within-release count/growth comparisons are valid for these defined city regions. Boundaries include non-urban land; do not treat their sum as national urban population or compare to national/DEGURBA urban percentages.
limitations
A deliberately dated teaching snapshot, not latest population data. Later revisions must not be mixed into this table. Missing national totals, urban classifications and land areas prevent urbanisation or density calculations.
