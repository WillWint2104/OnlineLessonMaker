Larger preview sizing and authored geographical component labels require a separately approved bounded integration task. Any future generic Geography Answer Hub composition needs separate bounded design/integration approval. Figma editing remains in the separate design chat; no editable Figma parity claim.

dot

status
Future opportunities only; no Dot dependency or modification
opportunities
Use Dot’s approved application to inspect the same dated population evidence at multiple scales, preserving source/legend/units and linked task context. Compare a population layer with dated transport, employment or environmental evidence to test a proposed mechanism; do not infer causation from overlay alone. If a future tool supports change, distinguish comparable dates/classifications from a single density snapshot.
integrationGate
Verify actual application capabilities, embedding/security/accessibility, data rights and teaching benefit before a new bounded task. No new GIS or map engine here.

blockers

  • Human pedagogical and visual approval of this actual candidate.
  • School-adopted syllabus and formal curriculum mapping must be confirmed before claiming alignment.
  • Separate explicit lesson-publication approval; no release date or corresponding video publication is authorised.

limitations

  • Paper responses are not automatically captured or marked; teacher-led feedback is required.
  • Generic standalone Geography questions are not indexed by the present practice-collection Answer Hub. Candidate final/worked policies are hidden, models are in the separate teacher guide, and all six answer keys are stripped from the standalone learner. No automatic completion reveal is promised.
  • Map is a reduced, adapted static official modelled grid: suitable for broad patterns, not exact household/cell measurements.
  • A dated release is deliberately used for internally consistent evidence, not latest population figures.
  • No physical-device, screen-reader or remote classroom-service certification; no observed classroom timing evidence.
  • No new smartphone development or polish.
