Where do Australia's refugees come from?

Offshore Humanitarian Program visas granted in 2023–24, by the visa-holder's country of citizenship. These are the conflicts and crises people are fleeing before they rebuild their lives in Australia.

4 in 5 of offshore grants went to people from just four countries — Afghanistan, Syria, Iraq and Myanmar — reflecting where the world's resettlement need was greatest.
Source: Dept. of Home Affairs, 2023–24 Offshore Humanitarian Program Outcomes (data extracted 8 July 2024). Top 10 citizenships of 16,750 offshore grants.
